About Shoprite

Shoprite is South Africa's largest food retailer, dedicated to delivering affordable, high-quality groceries and household goods to budget-conscious households across the country and continent.

The Market Problem We Solve

  • High Food Inflation: Offsetting rising cost-of-living pressure for vulnerable income groups.
  • Food Security: Guaranteeing steady access to nutritious bulk staples in urban and peri-urban regions.
  • Financial Inclusion: Offering accessible non-banking services via our Money Market counters.

The Shoprite Customer Journey

1. Discovery & Awareness

Customers discover deals through weekly WhatsApp alerts, digital flyers, local radio, or physical promotional leaflets.

2. Smart Budget Planning

Shoppers compare item prices and calculate Xtra Savings discounts upfront before entering the store.

3. In-Store / Digital Purchase

Items are purchased, and instant discounts are applied by swiping the Xtra Savings card or scanning the phone app at checkout.

4. Post-Purchase Value

Shoppers verify till-slip savings and top up prepaid utilities at the Money Market counter during the same visit.
